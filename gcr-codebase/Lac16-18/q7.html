The Importance of Good Typography

Good typography makes a webpage easier to read and creates a better experience for users. Choosing the right font, size, spacing and alignment can make content more attractive and understandable.

Font families can change the personality of a webpage. Serif fonts can provide a traditional appearance, while sans-serif fonts usually create a clean and modern look.

Font weight and size are also important. Headings can use larger and bolder text to attract attention, while normal paragraphs can use a comfortable font size for reading.

Proper line-height and letter-spacing improve readability. Text alignment also helps organize information and gives the page a balanced appearance.

Typography Tips

  • Choose readable font families.
  • Use suitable font sizes and weights.
  • Maintain comfortable line spacing.
  • Use spacing to separate important content.
